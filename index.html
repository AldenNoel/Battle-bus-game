Keyboard: WASD move, mouse look, click or F fire, right click aim, E open chest, Space jump or glider, R reload, Shift sprint (uses stamina), C slides, 1 pickaxe and 2-6 your five slots, E opens chests / picks up loot / drives a quad bike, X drops what you hold, B wall, V ramp, G grenade, Q quick-heal, M mutes. If your mouse is not captured, move it toward the screen edges to turn, or use the arrow keys.
Full screen: click the Full screen button on the menu (controller: Share). Your mouse is captured as soon as a match starts; press Esc to free it, click the game to capture it again.
PS5 controller: left stick move, right stick look, R2 fire, Cross jump or glider, Square open chest, L1/R1 switch weapon, L3 sprint, Circle or R3 slide, L2 aim, Square reload (or open a nearby chest), Triangle wall, D-pad up ramp, D-pad down grenade, D-pad right quick-heal, D-pad left drop item.
Touch: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, buttons on the right, tap a weapon slot to equip. Doors open on their own when you walk up.
